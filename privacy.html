Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

1. Who We Are

AEROEMOTION is a cinematic production service based in Spain, operated by Maksym ("we", "us"). We create lifestyle films for luxury real estate clients across the Costa Blanca and Valencia regions.

2. What Data We Collect

When you submit the contact form on our website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • A link to your property listing (optional)
  • Any additional message you choose to include (optional)

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools on this website. We do not collect data automatically beyond what your browser sends in standard HTTP requests (IP address, user agent).

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry via WhatsApp or email
  • Prepare a tailored visual concept for your property
  • Communicate about potential project collaboration

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

4. Data Processing

Your form submission is processed by Formspree, a US-based form backend service, which delivers the data to our email. Formspree acts as a data processor on our behalf. After submission, you may also be redirected to WhatsApp to send us a direct message — this is optional and handled by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) under their own privacy terms.

5. Legal Basis

We process your data based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when you voluntarily submit the contact form, and on our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and for up to 12 months afterward in case of follow-up. You may request deletion at any time.

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD)

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at aeroemotion.films@gmail.com.
